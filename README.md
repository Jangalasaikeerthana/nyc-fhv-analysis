# Albany Airbnb Market Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses Airbnb listing data from Albany, New York.
The goal was to understand how the short-term rental market is
distributed across Albany's neighbourhoods — who owns the listings,
what types of properties dominate, and whether commercial operators
have a significant presence in the market.

## Business Questions
- Which neighbourhood has the most Airbnb listings?
- What types of rooms are most commonly listed?
- Who are the top hosts by number of listings?
- How does room type distribution vary across neighbourhoods?
- Are listings dominated by individual hosts or commercial operators?

## Key Findings
- Sixth Ward dominates Albany's Airbnb market with 23.4% of all 
  listings — more than double any other neighbourhood.
- Sixth Ward shows a mixed hosting pattern — casual hosts with 
  1-2 properties coexist with commercial operators owning 7-15+ 
  listings. Entire home/apt accounts for 91.5% of Sixth Ward listings.
- Most listings across Albany are entire home/apt rentals, suggesting 
  dedicated rental properties rather than spare rooms.
- Tenth Ward is the only neighbourhood where private rooms outnumber 
  entire homes (30 vs 19). Investigation revealed this is largely 
  driven by a single commercial operator (Scott) who owns 13 private 
  room listings in that ward alone out of his 17 total listings.
